Polyfill for Gamepad Haptics PCM Extension

Controls:

  • Cross for Stereo PCM
  • Square for Left Handle PCM
  • Circle for Right Handle PCM
  • Triangle or D-pad Up for Reset Vibration
  • D-pad Left for Strong Rumble
  • D-pad Right for Weak Rumble
  • D-pad Down for Strong+Weak Rumble
  • Touchpad for Continuous Rumble (X = Strong magnitude, Y = Weak magnitude)
